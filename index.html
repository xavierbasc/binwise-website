Small rules, full shelves

One idea to learn, a hundred shelves to master.

Tap or drag

Tap a bin and then another, or drag the toys across. Whatever feels natural, with a finger or a mouse.

Same on same

Toys only land on their own kind or in an empty bin. Keep an empty bin as a bench and plan a few moves ahead.

Padlocks and wraps

Chained bins open when their toy is packed. Gift-wrapped toys only show what they are once they reach the top.

Undo, hint, retry

Undo even after running out of moves, get a hint from a real solver, and know that every shelf can be cleared.

How it works

One shelf at a time, one move at a time.

1

Look at the shelf

Bins of toys with little faces, all mixed up, and one or two empty bins to work with.

2

Move what is on top

The toys on top hop to another bin, onto the same toy or into an empty one.

3

Pack every kind

A full bin of one toy is sealed with a stamp. Seal them all to clear the shelf.

Questions

How many levels are there?

One hundred, in five tiers from tutorial to expert. Each one is generated from its number, so it is the same shelf every time you play it.

Can a level be impossible?

No. Every shelf is solved by the built-in solver before it is accepted, and the tests check all hundred.

Is there a timer or lives?

No timer and no lives. From level 21 there is a move limit, and undo works even after you run out.
